## Example

A switch acts right away (turn on notifications, dark mode). For a choice that only applies when a form is submitted, prefer a [checkbox](/docs/composants/case-a-cocher).

While sliding, the knob stretches towards its destination then shrinks back, and the cross it carries turns into a check mark.

## In a form

When on, the switch submits its `value` (`on` by default) under its `name`; when off, it submits nothing, like a checkbox.

<Callout>
  If the user asked their system to reduce motion, the knob jumps straight to the other side without stretching, and the check mark replaces the cross without transition.
</Callout>

## Accessibility

- Under the hood, a native checkbox with `role="switch"`: announced as "switch, on / off", toggled with Space. Its checked state provides `aria-checked`, with no attribute to keep in sync.
- `label` is required and visible; clicking it toggles the switch.
- The state does not rely on colour alone: the knob changes side and carries a check mark or a cross.
- The track stands out from the background with a contrast of at least 3:1, on and off; keyboard focus draws an outline around it.
