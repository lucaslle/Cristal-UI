## Example

`cr-confirm` wraps the button of an action one might regret. On click, the button gives way to a question (`question`) and two buttons: the one that confirms (`confirmLabel`, "Confirm" by default) and Cancel. The action runs in `onCrConfirm`.

## When to use it

For a one-off action in a list or a table row (delete a file, remove a member), the inline confirmation avoids interrupting the page. For a heavy action whose consequences need explaining, prefer a [dialog](/docs/composants/modale).

The confirm button carries the action verb ("Delete"), not a mere "Yes": users know what they accept without reading the question again.

<Callout>
  If the user asked their system to reduce motion, the question appears directly, without sliding.
</Callout>

## Accessibility

- The question and its two buttons form a group named by the question, read when focus enters it.
- Focus goes to Cancel: two quick presses on Enter are not enough to confirm by mistake.
- Escape or Cancel bring back the original button and move focus back to it; focus moving elsewhere also cancels the question.
- Inside a dialog, Escape cancels the question without closing the dialog.
