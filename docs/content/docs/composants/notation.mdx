## Example

`cr-rating` shows one star per possible rating, from 1 to `max` (5 by default), under a visible `label`. A click or the arrow keys pick the rating: stars fill in a wave up to it, and the chosen star bounces. On hover, the stars show the rating a click would give.

## In a form

The rating is submitted under the component `name`; nothing is submitted while no star is chosen (`value` at 0). `onCrChange` reports every new rating.

With `readonly`, the rating is displayed and cannot change, for an average for instance. Half stars are not supported: a decimal value is rounded.

<Callout>
  If the user asked their system to reduce motion, the stars fill all at once and the chosen star does not bounce.
</Callout>

## Accessibility

- The component follows the radio group pattern: a group named by the visible label, one radio button per star, named "3 stars out of 5", and a single Tab stop, on the chosen rating (or the first star).
- Right and Up Arrow raise the rating, Left and Down Arrow lower it, wrapping around after the last one; Home and End pick the lowest and the highest.
- When read-only, the stars form an image named by the rating ("Customer rating: 4 out of 5"), with nothing focusable.
- A full star is filled, an empty star only has its outline: the rating does not rely on colour alone. The outline stands out from the background with a contrast of at least 3:1.
