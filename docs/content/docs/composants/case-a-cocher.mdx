## In a form

A checked box submits its `value` (`on` by default) under its `name`; an unchecked box submits nothing, like a native checkbox. See the full example on the [Text field](/docs/composants/champ-texte#in-a-form) page.

`error-animation` animates the error when it appears and on every rejected submit (zigzag, badge, echo…): see [Animated error](/docs/composants/champ-texte#animated-error).

## Accessibility

- Native checkbox under the hood: checked with Space, announced as "checkbox, checked / not checked".
- The label is clickable and enlarges the click area.
- `indeterminate` is announced as "partially checked".
