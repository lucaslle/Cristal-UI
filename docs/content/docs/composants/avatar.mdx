## Example

`cr-avatar` shows a person's photo (`src`) or, without one, their initials: the first letter of the first and last words of `name`. If the photo fails to load, the initials replace it. The background colour comes from the name: the same person keeps the same colour everywhere. It is derived from the theme primary colour with a hue shift, dark enough for the white initials to keep a contrast of at least 4.5:1.

`size` sets the size (`s` 30px, `m` 40px, `l` 48px). `status` adds an availability badge: `online` (filled, with a check mark), `away` (a clock), `busy` (a bar), `offline` (an empty ring). The online badge pulses three times when it appears, then stops.

## Group

`cr-avatar-group` overlaps the `cr-avatar` elements it contains; they fan out on hover, or when focus is inside the group. With `max`, only the first avatars are shown, and a "+N" badge sums up the others.

## Accessibility

- Each avatar is an image named by the person, status included: "Mary Smith, online". The initials and the photo are not read on top of it.
- The status does not rely on colour alone: each status has its own shape (check mark, clock, bar, empty ring), and it is part of the name read aloud.
- The online badge pulse lasts less than 5 seconds, then stops: nothing moves continuously.
- The "+N" badge of a group is an image named "2 more people"; hidden avatars are not read.
