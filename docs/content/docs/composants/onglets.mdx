## Example

Each tab is a `CrTabPanel` child: its `label` becomes the tab, its content the panel. `selected` is the index of the chosen tab (0 by default); `onCrChange` returns the new index.

A bar slides under the chosen tab with a slight spring, and the content comes in from the side of the chosen tab: from the right when moving forward, from the left when moving back.

## Server rendering

Panels that are not chosen are hidden by the component itself: imported from `@cristal/react/ssr`, tabs arrive in the right state, with no flash of the other panels.

<Callout>
  If the user asked their system to reduce motion, the bar jumps straight under the chosen tab and the content fades in without sliding.
</Callout>

## Accessibility

- The component follows the W3C tabs pattern: `role="tablist"`, one `role="tab"` per tab with `aria-selected` and `aria-controls`, and a `role="tabpanel"` named by its tab (`aria-labelledby`).
- A single Tab stop enters the tab bar, on the chosen tab; one more Tab goes into the panel.
- Automatic activation: Right Arrow and Left Arrow pick the next or previous tab (wrapping), Home and End the first or last one.
- The chosen tab does not rely on colour alone: the thick bar is under it.
