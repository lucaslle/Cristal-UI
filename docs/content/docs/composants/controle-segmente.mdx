The control has two modes, picked by its children:

- `option` elements: a radio group, whose value can be sent with a form;
- `CrTabPanel` elements: tabs, each with its content panel, one visible at a time.

The four `variant` values and `fit` apply to both modes.

## Example

Options are child `option` elements, as with `CrSelect`. A thumb slides under the chosen option with a slight spring.

| `variant` | Effect |
|---|---|
| `thumb` | Sliding Thumb: a raised thumb, the chosen label goes bold (default) |
| `clip` | Clip Text: a coloured thumb; while it slides, text turns white only where it covers it |
| `underline` | no track: a bar slides under the chosen label, the classic tabs look |
| `outline` | bordered track, tinted thumb ringed with the primary colour |

## Fitted width

By default, segments all have the same width. With `fit`, each one hugs its label and the thumb resizes as it slides. A `disabled` option cannot be picked.

## Tabs

With `CrTabPanel` children instead of `option` elements, the control becomes tabs: each panel's `label` becomes a tab, its content the panel. `value` names the chosen tab by its panel's `value`, or else by its position as a string (`"0"`, `"1"`…); the first tab by default. `onCrChange` returns that value. In tabs mode, the control sends nothing with a form.

The content comes in from the side of the chosen tab: from the right when moving forward, from the left when moving back. With `underline`, the base line runs the full width; add `fit` for tabs that hug their labels.

Panels that are not chosen are hidden by the component itself: imported from `@cristal/react/ssr`, tabs arrive in the right state, with no flash of the other panels.

<Callout>
  If the user asked their system to reduce motion, the thumb jumps straight to the chosen option or tab, counts do not move and the panel appears without sliding.
</Callout>

## Accessibility

### With options

- The control is a radio group (`role="radiogroup"`) named by `label`, which is required since there is no visible label.
- A single Tab stop enters the group, on the chosen option; arrow keys pick the previous or next option (wrapping), Home and End the first or last one.
- The count is read with the label ("Month (38)"); the counting number is not read.
- With `name`, the chosen value is sent with the form.

### With tabs

- The component follows the W3C tabs pattern: `role="tablist"` named by `label`, one `role="tab"` per tab with `aria-selected` and `aria-controls`, and a `role="tabpanel"` named by its tab (`aria-labelledby`).
- A single Tab stop enters the tab bar, on the chosen tab; one more Tab goes into the panel.
- Automatic activation: Right Arrow and Left Arrow pick the next or previous tab (wrapping), Home and End the first or last one.
- The chosen tab does not rely on colour alone: the thumb or the thick bar is under it.
