## Example

Write the levels as they are inside `cr-breadcrumb`: `<a href>` links (or your router's `Link`, which renders an `<a>`), then the current page last, in a `<span>`. The links stay yours, in the page: Ctrl/Cmd+click, middle click and "Open in new tab" are the browser's own. Chevrons separate the levels.

Beyond `max` levels (4 by default), the middle ones collapse behind a "…" button: only the first one and the last `max - 2` stay visible. Clicking "…" reveals the hidden levels, which slide out of it one after the other.

## Accessibility

- The breadcrumb is a navigation region (`nav`) named "Breadcrumb", with an ordered list: one list item per level.
- The last level gets `aria-current="page"`; it also stands out without colour, since it is not a link (no underline).
- Separators are drawn in CSS, without text: screen readers do not read them.
- The "…" button is named by what it does ("Show 3 hidden levels") and has `aria-expanded`. Once the levels are revealed, it goes away and focus moves to the first revealed link.
