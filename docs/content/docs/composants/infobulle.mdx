## Example

`cr-tooltip` wraps a single focusable element (native button or link, `cr-button`) and adds a short help text to it, `text`. The tooltip grows out of the side set by `placement` (`top` by default, `bottom`, `start` or `end`) with a slight bounce.

For an icon-only button, use the [icon button](/docs/composants/bouton-icone) instead: it already shows its label as a tooltip. A tooltip only holds text, with no link or button.

<Callout>
  If the user asked their system to reduce motion, the tooltip appears at full size straight away, without growing.
</Callout>

## Accessibility

- W3C tooltip pattern: `role="tooltip"`, and the text describes the trigger (read after its name). Since an id cannot cross the shadow DOM, the description goes through `aria-description` on the trigger.
- The tooltip opens immediately on keyboard focus and after 0.3 s on hover; it can be hovered without disappearing, and Escape closes it even when focus is elsewhere (WCAG criterion 1.4.13).
- No tooltip after a simple tap on a touch screen or after a click: the information must not rely on the tooltip alone.
- Light text on a dark background, contrast above 4.5:1; visible border in high contrast mode.
