## Example

The field starts at `rows` lines (1 by default) and grows one line at a time, with a slight bounce, up to `maxRows` (8 by default); beyond that, it scrolls. The `end` slot places a button at the bottom right, for example to send.

## Keyboard

Enter inserts a new line; Ctrl + Enter (Cmd + Enter on Mac) submits the form.

`error-animation` animates the error when it appears and on every rejected submit (zigzag, badge, echo…): see [Animated error](/docs/composants/champ-texte#animated-error).
