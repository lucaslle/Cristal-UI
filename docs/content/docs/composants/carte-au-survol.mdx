## Example

`cr-hover-card` wraps a link (a name, a page, a product) and shows a preview of its destination when the pointer lingers on it for half a second (`openDelay`, in milliseconds) or keyboard focus reaches it. The card content goes in `slot="card"`.

## What goes in it

The card is a preview: everything it shows must also be found behind the link, since it cannot be seen with a finger and screen readers do not read it. It holds no button or link: they could not be reached with the keyboard. For a panel with actions, use a [popover](/docs/composants/fenetre-contextuelle); for short help text, a [tooltip](/docs/composants/infobulle).

<Callout>
  If the user asked their system to reduce motion, the card appears directly, without unfolding.
</Callout>

## Accessibility

- The card opens on keyboard focus as on hover, and stays open while the pointer moves from the link to the card.
- Escape closes it, wherever focus is; it also closes when focus leaves the link.
- With a finger, a tap follows the link without opening the card.
