Add four components: cr-confirm (a risky action's button turns into an inline question with Confirm and Cancel, focus on Cancel, Escape restores the button), cr-hover-card (link preview opened on hover after a delay or on keyboard focus, hoverable, closed with Escape), cr-code-block (code with a Copy button announced to screen readers, keyboard-scrollable) and cr-kbd (keys and shortcuts, Mod shown as ⌘ on Mac and Ctrl elsewhere). New token `--cr-font-mono`.
