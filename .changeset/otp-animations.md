Add an `animation` prop to cr-otp: pop (default, unchanged), bounce (the digit drops in, the box squashes and bounces back, boxes hop when complete), stretch (a single focus ring stretches from box to box, then wraps the whole code), spin (digits roll in like a slot-machine reel and spin again in a wave when complete), merge (when complete, the boxes slide together into one rounded success bar), zoom (the digit zooms in from big, a shine sweeps across when complete) and tint (the box fills with colour from the bottom, turning green when complete). A pasted code arrives as a quick cascade, Backspace undoes the complete state, nothing moves when less motion is requested, and the single real input stays the only control screen readers see.
