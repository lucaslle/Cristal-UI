Add cr-avatar and cr-avatar-group. cr-avatar shows a photo, or initials on a colour derived from the name (hue shifts of the primary colour, white text kept at 4.5:1 or more) when there is no photo or it fails to load; it is an image named by the person, with an optional status (online, away, busy, offline) added to the name and shown by a badge whose shape changes with the status. The online badge pulses three times then stops. cr-avatar-group overlaps its avatars, fans them out on hover or focus, and sums up those beyond max with a "+N" named "N more people".
