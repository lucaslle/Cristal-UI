Add cr-slider, a form-associated range input built on a native input type="range" (keyboard and slider role for free) with a visible label, min, max, step, crInput while dragging and crChange on release. The filled part of the track follows the value, and a value bubble follows the thumb and grows while dragging or focused, without transition when less motion is requested.
