Built-in texts (validation messages, stepper buttons, table and dashboard announcements, close labels, search and card captions) now come in English and French. English is the default; French is used when the component or an ancestor has lang="fr". closeLabel (cr-dialog, cr-drawer) and label (cr-stepper, cr-dashboard) no longer default to French text: when unset they use the translated default.
