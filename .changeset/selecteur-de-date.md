Add cr-date-picker, a form-associated date field: typing in the page language format (DD/MM/YYYY in French, MM/DD/YYYY in English, shown as a hint) or picking from a calendar dialog following the WAI-ARIA APG Date Picker Dialog pattern (arrows by day and week, Page Up/Down by month, Shift for a year, Home/End within the week, Escape returns focus to the button). ISO value, min, max and required with built-in validation messages; month and weekday names come from Intl, weeks start on Monday in French. Months slide in from the side of navigation and the selected day pops, without motion when less motion is requested.
