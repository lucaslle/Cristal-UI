Add cr-search, an animated search field with four variants (outline, filled, underline, and expand where a round magnifier button stretches into the field): the magnifier morphs into a clear arrow on focus, placeholder words roll (at most four times), recent searches show as chips that fly into the field, and a "no results" state where the magnifier shakes and a "?" pops up. cr-loader gains variant="search", a gliding magnifier.
