Add cr-tooltip, a short text hint around a button, a link or a cr-button following the W3C tooltip pattern: shown on keyboard focus and after a short hover delay, hoverable, closed with Escape from anywhere, and exposed as the trigger's description. It grows out of the trigger side with a slight spring, at full size straight away when less motion is requested. The cr-icon-button tooltip now shares the same styles and motion.
