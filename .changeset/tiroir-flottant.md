cr-drawer gets `variant="floating"`: the panel floats away from the screen edges with rounded corners. With `movable`, users move it by dragging its header, or with the arrow keys on its handle (Shift for bigger steps); with `resizable`, they resize it from its bottom corner on the page side, with the pointer or the arrow keys (minimum 280 × 200 px). It stays on screen and gets back its edge and size when it closes.
