Add cr-popover, a small non-modal panel (role="dialog") anchored to its button, for interactive content: aria-expanded and aria-controls on the button, focus moved into the panel on opening and back to the button on closing, closed by Escape, a click outside, tabbing out or setting open to false, with crToggle for user changes. It scales out of the button with a spring, and simply appears when less motion is requested.
